Seed-Spreader Drone

A standard spraying hexacopter with three added parts: a seed hopper, a metering gate and a spinner disc. The same drone that sprays crops can re-seed the field after harvest.

Simulation assumptions: 60 × 60 m field (0.36 ha), 25 kg hopper, 10-second refill stop at the pad, 3 m working height. These are adjustable estimates for the class MOC, not measured data. The drone is modelled from scratch to resemble a typical agricultural hexacopter.
